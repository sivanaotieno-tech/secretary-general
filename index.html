01 / ABOUT

A Secretary General should make things clearer.

The Secretary General role depends on communication, organisation, reliability, and making sure ideas move from discussion to action.

This campaign is built around a simple approach: listen to students, communicate what is happening, organise information, and report back on progress.

05 / FAQ

Questions worth asking.

What would the Secretary General actually do?

The exact duties depend on your school's student-government structure. In general, the role can involve coordinating meetings, communicating decisions, keeping records, and helping student leaders work together.

How will student feedback be handled?

Feedback can be collected through agreed school channels, grouped by topic, and followed by a clear update about what can be acted on and what requires further discussion or approval.

What happens if an idea cannot be implemented?

The commitment is to communicate that clearly rather than promise something outside the office's authority. Students should know the reason, the next step, or whether the issue needs to be taken elsewhere.

What makes this different from a list of promises?

The emphasis is on a process: listen, organise, communicate, act where possible, and report back. The details should be adapted to the school's rules and what the office can actually control.
